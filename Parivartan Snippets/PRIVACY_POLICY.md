# Privacy Policy for Parivartan Snippet

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Parivartan Snippet ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Usage

* **Locally Stored Data:** All shortcuts, snippets, templates, folder structures, and configuration settings created in Parivartan Snippet are stored **strictly on the user's local device** using the browser's native `chrome.storage.local` API.
* **No Remote Transmission:** We do **not** transmit, sell, rent, or monetize your snippet text, shortcuts, or usage data to any third-party servers, advertisers, or data brokers.
* **No Analytics or Telemetry:** Parivartan Snippet does not employ tracking pixels, Google Analytics, or third-party user monitoring libraries.

---

### 2. Browser Permissions Justification

In accordance with Google Chrome Web Store and Mozilla Add-on developer policies, we only request the minimal permissions necessary for the extension to function:

* **`storage`**: Used exclusively to save and retrieve your snippets, folders, and preferences locally on your computer.
* **`contextMenus`**: Used to provide the right-click menu options ("Open Parivartan Snippet Dashboard" and "Save selection as Parivartan Snippet").
* **`activeTab`**: Used strictly to detect when a typed shortcut matches an existing snippet and to replace the shortcut with your template text in the active input field.

---

### 3. Data Retention and Deletion

* Because all data resides locally on your computer, you have complete ownership and control over your information at all times.
* You can export a full backup of your snippets at any time via the **"Export JSON"** button in the dashboard.
* Uninstalling the extension from your browser immediately and permanently removes all stored snippets and settings from your machine.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy

Parivartan Snippet does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Contact Information

If you have questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or the security of Parivartan Snippet, please contact our support team or file an issue on our official repository.
